# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different experience levels. Critique the work, not the person. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, reveal private information, or post unwanted promotional material.

Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project. Share only fictional test data and respect the privacy of users, customers, and other contributors. Do not post production HAR captures or live credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the project and its community. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through an available private GitHub channel, or post a minimal request for a private contact method without disclosing the sensitive details publicly.

This project does not promise a staffed moderation service or a particular response time.
